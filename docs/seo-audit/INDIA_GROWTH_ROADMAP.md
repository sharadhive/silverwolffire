## 3. India-Specific Buyer Trust Anchors

To win against generic agencies, on-page copy and buyer guides must address the exact realities of Indian commerce:

1. **GST Compliance & Invoicing:**
   - Clearly state that all services include GST-compliant tax invoices (SAC codes, input tax credit eligibility).
2. **Standard INR Pricing Factors:**
   - Provide transparent cost drivers for Indian clients: explain that marketing websites typically range between ₹15,000 and ₹75,000, while complex custom web applications and CRMs range between ₹1,00,000 and ₹5,00,000+ depending on database architecture and third-party integrations.
3. **Regional Communication & Escalation:**
   - Emphasize fluent English, Hindi, and Marathi support, with real-time Slack/WhatsApp working channels during Indian Standard Time (IST) working hours (9:00 AM – 6:30 PM IST).
4. **Code & IP Ownership:**
   - Guarantee 100% intellectual property and source code handover upon final milestone clearance — eliminating the fear of vendor lock-in common with Indian freelance agencies.
